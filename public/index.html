GRAM-PULSE is a hackathon prototype for decision support. Data shown in this demonstration is simulated unless explicitly marked otherwise. Model outputs are indicative and should support, not replace, official field verification and administrative decision-making. Existing rural GIS systems provide mapping and planning capabilities; GRAM-PULSE proposes a predictive, dependency-aware simulation layer to anticipate cascading failures and optimize interventions under constrained Panchayat budgets.
